Questions

Does it work on my phone?

It opens in the Google Sheets and Excel apps, but it's easiest to use on a computer or tablet.

Can I change the services and categories?

Yes. Edit the lists on the Setup tab and every dropdown updates.

I do pet sitting, not just walks. Does it fit?

Yes. Overnight and day-long sits, drop-ins and extra pets are already in the services list, and you can add your own.

Is this tax advice?

No. It organises your numbers and suggests a savings amount for tax based on the percentage you choose. Check the rules where you live or ask an accountant.
