Prepaid packs, counted for you

Log the pack once when you sell it. Each time the client trains, pick the pack on the Sessions tab and leave the price at 0. The Packages tab shows sessions used and left, and flags packs that are Low (2 or fewer left), Used up or Expired.

  • The pack sale counts as revenue once, so nothing is double-counted
  • See which clients are running low, handy for rebooking
  • Pack names, sizes and prices are all editable

Questions

Does it work on my phone?

It opens in the Google Sheets and Excel apps, but it's easiest to use on a computer or tablet.

I don't sell packs. Is it still useful?

Yes. Leave the Packages tab empty and log each session with its price; everything else works the same.

Can I change the services, packs and categories?

Yes. Edit the lists on the Setup tab and every dropdown updates.

Is this tax advice?

No. It organises your numbers and suggests a savings amount for tax based on the percentage you choose. Check the rules where you live or ask an accountant.
